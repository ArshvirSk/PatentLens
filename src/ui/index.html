How PatentLens Works

A step-by-step explanation of the IR pipeline, mapped to the CSDC7023 syllabus.

1

Corpus Ingestion

Module 1 — Introduction to IR

Raw patent documents (JSON) are parsed into structured zones: Title, Abstract, Claims, and Description. Each zone carries different semantic weight — claims are the legal core of a patent, while descriptions provide supporting detail.

2

Text Preprocessing

Module 3 — Query Operations

Each zone's text is tokenized, converted to lowercase, stripped of stopwords (common English words like "the", "and"), and then stemmed using the Porter Stemmer to reduce words to their root form (e.g., "processing" → "process").

3

Inverted Index

Module 4 — Indexing & Scoring

A per-zone inverted index is built: for each stemmed term in each zone, we store a postings list mapping document IDs to their term frequencies. This enables sub-second lookup of all documents containing any query term.

4

TF-IDF Weighting

Module 4 — Indexing & Scoring

Term importance is computed as w(t,d) = (1 + log₁₀(tf)) × log₁₀(N / df). Terms that appear frequently in a document (high TF) but rarely across the corpus (high IDF) receive the highest weights. Document vector lengths are precomputed for normalization.

5

Vector Space Model & Cosine Similarity

Module 4 — Indexing & Scoring

Both the query and each document are represented as TF-IDF weighted vectors. The cosine similarity between them measures relevance: cos(θ) = (q⃗ · d⃗) / (|q⃗| × |d⃗|). This is computed independently per zone.

6

Weighted Zone Scoring

Module 4 — Weighted Zone Scoring

Per-zone cosine scores are combined with configurable weights: score = w₁·sim(claims) + w₂·sim(title) + w₃·sim(abstract) + w₄·sim(description). Default: Claims 0.4, Title 0.3, Abstract 0.2, Description 0.1 — reflecting the legal importance hierarchy of patent sections.

7

Top-K Ranking & Boolean Filtering

Module 3 & 4 — Queries & Scoring

Documents are ranked by their final weighted score and the Top-K are returned. Optional Boolean pre-filters on CPC class and date range narrow the candidate set before ranking (structured query support from Module 3).

8

Evaluation Metrics

Module 5 — Evaluation of IR Systems

Retrieval quality is measured using standard IR metrics: Precision@K (fraction of top-K that are relevant), Recall@K (fraction of relevant found in top-K), F1@K, and Mean Average Precision (MAP) across the full query set. Patent citation graphs serve as ground truth.
